# Zaeir Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Zaeir is a travel guide for Morocco. This page explains what the app does with your data. Short version: the app does not collect, send or share your personal data.

## What stays on your phone
- Your language choice, trip plan, favourite places and trip notes are saved on your phone only. We never receive them.
- If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

## Location
- The app asks for location permission only if you use "Near me", to show which cities are closest to you.
- Your position is used on your phone at that moment. It is not stored by us, not sent to any server and not shared.
- You can refuse or remove the permission at any time in Android settings. The rest of the app works without it.

## Internet and third parties
- The guide content and photos are inside the app. The app itself does not connect to any server and has no accounts, ads or analytics.
- Some buttons open other websites or apps in your browser (for example Booking.com, Airbnb or ONCF). Those sites have their own privacy policies. We do not control them.
- "Share my trip" uses the Android share menu. You choose where the text goes. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

## Children
Zaeir is a general travel guide and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
